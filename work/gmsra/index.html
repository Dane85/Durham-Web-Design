The members are retirees and their spouses, reading on everything from desktops to tablets to phones. Every decision on this site starts with one question: can a member read this and do what they came to do, first time, without help?

Visit the live site
The challenge

Make it easy to read, easy to join, easy to keep up to date.

Members mostly want three things: when and where the next gathering is, what's coming up, and how to join or renew. Small grey text, cluttered menus and forms that ask too much get in the way, especially for older readers.

The association's volunteers also needed a site they wouldn't have to babysit. Events shouldn't have to be typed in twice, and membership applications shouldn't get lost in someone's inbox.

What I built

Simple on the outside, looked after on the inside.

Large, high-contrast text

An 18px base size, strong contrast, clear headings and big buttons. Members can read every page without zooming in.

The next gathering, always in view

The date and place of the next meeting sit at the top of every page, and the events list updates itself from the association's own calendar.

Join or renew online

A short membership form, with each application stored securely for the executive. A printable form, mail and e-transfer are still offered for members who prefer them.

Photo galleries

Galleries from the summer BBQ and the Vic Pratt Memorial Golf Tournament, with a viewer that works with a mouse, the keyboard or a swipe.

Fast and lightweight

A theme written from scratch, with no page builder or plugin bloat, so pages respond in about a third of a second.

Hosted and cared for

I host, monitor and maintain the site. Changes are tried on a private copy of the site first, then go live, and accessibility is reviewed as the site grows.
