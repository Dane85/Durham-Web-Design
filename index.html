Questions

Good questions to ask any web designer.

How does payment work?

A 50% deposit gets your project started, and the rest is due when your site is ready to launch. You can pay the deposit by card through Stripe. The monthly care plan begins once your site is live. All prices are in Canadian dollars, plus 13% HST.

What does the monthly care plan cover?

Hosting, your SSL certificate (the padlock), security and software updates, nightly backups, and fixing anything that breaks. Growth Business plans also include an hour of content changes every month.

Where is my website hosted?

On a dedicated cloud server, provided by Hetzner and managed by me. It isn't a crowded shared hosting plan. It's located in Virginia, USA, with a fast connection to Ontario. If your business needs data stored in Canada, tell me and we'll plan for it.

Do you use WordPress?

Not by default. I write lean, custom code, which keeps sites fast and leaves very little to break or patch. If you want to edit content yourself, we'll pick a setup that suits you.

Can you fix or speed up my current website?

Usually, yes. Start with the free website check, or send me your address through the form below. I'll tell you honestly whether a fix or a rebuild makes more sense.

How long does a website take?

Most small business sites take a few weeks from deposit to launch. A lot depends on how quickly content and feedback come together. Custom builds take longer, and you'll get a timeline with your quote.
